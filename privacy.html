Privacy Policy

How we handle information shared through the website and our contact channels.

Last updated: 13 October 2026
Who we areInformation receivedHow we use itWebsite technologySharing and transfersRetention and securityYour requestsContact and updates

This notice describes personal information connected with visits to www.tga-uae.com and enquiries sent to TGA. The public website has no account registration or on-site enquiry form.

1. Who we are

TGA Manangement Consultancy LLC is the point of contact for personal information described in this notice. Our office is at Office 105, Al Jawhara Building, Mankhool, Dubai, UAE.

2. Information we receive

If you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, contact information, organisation and enquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive documents until we have agreed a suitable channel and scope for your matter.

Like most websites, the hosting service may record technical details needed to deliver and protect pages, such as an IP address, browser information, requested page and time of access.

3. How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond, understand the services requested, arrange a follow-up and, where a separate engagement is agreed, administer that relationship. Technical information may be used to operate, troubleshoot and secure the website. We may also retain information where applicable legal or professional obligations require it.

4. Website technology and external services

The current website does not run an on-site enquiry form, advertising pixels or analytics scripts. It does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. The stylesheet requests DM Sans and Manrope fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive technical request information. The Contact page embeds Google Maps; loading the map may send technical information to Google and may involve Google cookies under its privacy policy. If you select the WhatsApp link, your interaction takes place through WhatsApp and is governed by its own privacy information.

5. Sharing and international services

Information may be accessible to TGA personnel who handle your enquiry and to providers that support website hosting, email or messaging. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect legitimate rights. Some providers may process information outside the UAE; their own policies and applicable safeguards govern those services.

6. Retention and security

We keep information for the period reasonably needed for its purpose, any agreed client relationship and applicable recordkeeping duties. Access is limited according to business need, and we use reasonable measures to protect information. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your requests

You may contact us to ask about information relating to you, request a correction or raise a concern about its use. Requests for access, deletion, restriction or withdrawal of consent will be considered under applicable law and any obligations to retain records. The UAE Government’s data protection overview provides background on the UAE framework.

8. Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, email kintu@tga-uae.com or write to our Dubai office above. We may revise this notice if our website or handling practices change; the date above indicates the latest version.
