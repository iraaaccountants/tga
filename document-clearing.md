# Document Clearing, Attestation & Translation

Coordinate official documents, attestation, translation and notary steps through the appropriate providers.

## What we cover

- [Certificate Attestation Assistance](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/pro-visas/document-clearing#certificate-attestation): Organise education, birth, marriage and commercial documents for the relevant attestation route.
- [Certified Legal Translation Coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/pro-visas/document-clearing#legal-translation): Coordinate documents with a qualified legal translation provider where a certified translation is required.
- [Notarisation Coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/pro-visas/document-clearing#notarisation): Prepare document information and appointments for the appropriate public or authorised notary.
- [POA Registration Assistance](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/pro-visas/poa-registration-assistance): Coordinate power of attorney information, specialist drafting when needed and notary registration.

TGA provides application and document coordination. The relevant authority or qualified provider decides on approvals and legal documents.
