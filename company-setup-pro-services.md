# Company Setup & PRO Services in Dubai

> Move from a company plan to the documents and coordination needed for formation and ongoing corporate administration.

Category: Business setup & PRO services.

## Overview

Company formation involves a sequence of decisions, forms and supporting documents. A clear plan helps reduce avoidable rework when different parties need to provide information or sign documents.
TGA supports company setup and PRO processes in the UAE. We help gather the relevant business details, organise application materials and coordinate practical steps with the requirements of the chosen authority in view.

## Where TGA can help

- **Formation information:** Bring together activity, ownership, management and contact details needed to prepare an application.
- **Application coordination:** Track the required forms, supporting documents and decisions through the relevant setup process.
- **PRO support:** Assist with the administrative processes connected to the business and its approved setup route.

## How we work

1. **Confirm the setup route:** We review the business plan and chosen authority requirements.
2. **Prepare the file:** We organise the requested information and documents.
3. **Coordinate next steps:** We support the agreed applications and follow-up work.

## Useful information to share

- Proposed trade name and activities
- Shareholder and manager identification
- Preferred jurisdiction or location
- Target launch timing and existing approvals

The authority determines documentation, processing and approval requirements. TGA’s role is scoped to the assistance agreed for your setup.
