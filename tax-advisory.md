# UAE Tax Advisory Services in Dubai

> Work through a tax question using the facts, records and planned actions of your business.

Category: UAE tax advisory & transfer pricing.

## Overview

Tax questions often arise before a transaction, change in structure or filing. The answer depends on what the business actually does, how a transaction is documented and which rules apply at the time.
TGA provides tax advisory support. We help define the issue, review relevant information and explain the considerations that management should address before deciding what to do next.

## Where TGA can help

- **Question framing:** State the transaction, decision or tax position that needs review.
- **Fact gathering:** Collect the contracts, financial information and prior treatment relevant to the question.
- **Options and implications:** Set out the considerations, uncertainties and follow-up work for management.

## How we work

1. **Define the issue:** We agree exactly what needs an answer.
2. **Review the facts:** We examine the information relevant to the analysis.
3. **Discuss the result:** We explain the practical implications and next steps.

## Useful information to share

- A concise description of the tax question
- Relevant contracts and invoices
- Business and group structure
- Existing filings or correspondence

Tax outcomes depend on current law and complete facts. Advice should be reviewed when either changes.
