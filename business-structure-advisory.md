# Business Structure Advisory in Dubai

> Make the early decisions about activity, ownership and operating model before choosing a UAE setup route.

Category: Business setup & PRO services.

## Overview

A company structure should support what the business actually plans to do. Before an application is prepared, it helps to be clear about the proposed activities, where the business will operate, who will own it and what the owners expect to change later.
TGA’s business structure advisory starts with those facts. We help organise the questions that shape a setup decision and discuss the practical implications of available routes. The appropriate route depends on the activity and requirements of the relevant authority.

## Where TGA can help

- **Activity and operations:** Define the proposed activities, customer base and day-to-day operating needs that a setup route must accommodate.
- **Ownership and governance:** Discuss shareholders, management responsibilities and the documents needed to describe the intended structure.
- **Setup route:** Compare relevant jurisdiction and licensing considerations before moving into company formation work.

## How we work

1. **Discuss the plan:** We establish the intended activities and commercial priorities.
2. **Map the decisions:** We identify the structure and licensing questions that need answers.
3. **Agree a route:** We explain the next setup steps and the information required to proceed.

## Useful information to share

- Proposed business activities
- Owners and expected ownership shares
- Where customers and operations will be based
- Existing group or company documents, if applicable

Licensing and ownership requirements depend on the selected activity and authority. A structure should be confirmed against current requirements before an application is filed.
