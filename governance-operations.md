# Governance & operations

Organise the policies, controls and management information that help a business operate consistently.

## What we cover

- [Internal controls review](https://www.tga-uae.com/governance-operations.html#internal-controls-review): Review approval, reconciliation and recordkeeping practices and identify practical improvements.
- [Policy & procedure writing](https://www.tga-uae.com/governance-operations.html#policies-procedures): Document clear responsibilities and steps for recurring business processes.
- [Board reporting packs](https://www.tga-uae.com/governance-operations.html#board-reporting): Bring financial and operational information together for leadership review.
- [Compliance calendars](https://www.tga-uae.com/governance-operations.html#compliance-calendar): Track known filing, renewal and review dates in one working schedule.
- [Document retention processes](https://www.tga-uae.com/governance-operations.html#document-retention): Organise where key records are kept and how they can be retrieved.
- [Business process reviews](https://www.tga-uae.com/governance-operations.html#business-process-review): Map a process, identify bottlenecks and agree practical improvements.
- [Transaction due diligence support](https://www.tga-uae.com/governance-operations.html#transaction-due-diligence): Organise financial and operational information for an acquisition or sale review.
