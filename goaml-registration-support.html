AML compliance assistance

goAML Registration Support in the UAE

Get the entity information, authorised user details and documents ready for a clearer goAML registration process.

Discuss this service ↗

Overview

Support shaped around your business.

The goAML platform is used by reporting entities to communicate with the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit. Registration requirements depend on the entity's activities and supervisory authority.

TGA can help review the starting position, organise the information requested for registration and support the authorised user through the setup steps. The reporting entity retains responsibility for its account and submissions.

What we cover

Where we can help.

01

Registration scope

Review the business activity, relevant supervisory body and existing account status.

02

Application information

Collect trade licence, entity and authorised representative details for the application.

03

Access and handover

Support the registration workflow and organise the account information for the responsible team.

How we work

Getting started

Useful information to share

  • Trade licence and legal entity details
  • Supervisory body and relevant activity
  • Authorised user's identification and contact details
  • Any existing SACM or goAML registration correspondence

Whether registration is required depends on the entity's activities and regulator. Portal access and reporting remain with the authorised reporting entity.

Official guidance: Ministry of Economy and Tourism goAML registration guidance
