# Labour & MOHRE Transactions

Employer paperwork and follow-up support for work permits, contracts and labour administration.

## What we cover

- [Work Permit & Contract Assistance](https://www.tga-uae.com/labour-mohre#work-permits-contracts): Organise information for work permit applications, renewals and employment contract transactions.
- [Employee Quota Assistance](https://www.tga-uae.com/labour-mohre#employee-quota): Prepare employer details for an applicable quota request or adjustment.
- [Labour Cancellation Paperwork](https://www.tga-uae.com/labour-mohre#labour-cancellations): Coordinate the documents and steps for an agreed permit or contract cancellation.
- [WPS File Updates](https://www.tga-uae.com/payroll-hr#wps-support): Prepare payroll information for an agreed Wage Protection System file update.

TGA provides application and document coordination. The relevant authority or qualified provider decides on approvals and legal documents.
