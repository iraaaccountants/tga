# Tax Registration & Annual Filing Support in the UAE

> Organise tax registrations and recurring filing work around the obligations relevant to your business.

Category: UAE tax advisory & transfer pricing.

## Overview

Different taxes and business circumstances can create different registration or filing tasks. A useful starting point is to identify the entity, its activities, the tax period and what has already been submitted.
TGA supports tax registration and annual filing work. We help bring together company details, financial information and prior records so the applicable task can be reviewed and prepared. For VAT and Corporate Tax, see the dedicated service pages for more focused information.

## Where TGA can help

- **Registration position:** Review the business details and tax registrations that may be relevant to the matter.
- **Filing calendar:** Identify the periods, prior filings and records needed for the upcoming work.
- **Submission preparation:** Organise the information and review questions before an applicable filing is completed.

## How we work

1. **Identify the obligation:** We clarify the tax type and period involved.
2. **Collect the information:** We list the records and decisions required.
3. **Prepare the work:** We agree the scope of registration or filing assistance.

## Useful information to share

- Trade licence and entity details
- Existing tax registration numbers
- Prior tax filings and notices
- Financial records for the relevant period

The applicable registration or filing rules depend on the tax type, taxpayer and current FTA requirements.
