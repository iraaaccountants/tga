Overview

Support shaped around the matter at hand.

Company formation involves a sequence of decisions, forms and supporting documents. A clear plan helps reduce avoidable rework when different parties need to provide information or sign documents.

TGA supports company setup and PRO processes in the UAE. We help gather the relevant business details, organise application materials and coordinate practical steps with the requirements of the chosen authority in view.
