# UAE business setup & PRO services

> From choosing a suitable structure to coordinating setup requirements, TGA helps founders and established businesses make the next step clear.

Build on the right structure.

Setting up a company involves more than completing an application. Your proposed activity, ownership, operating plans and preferred location all affect the questions you need to resolve before formation.
TGA combines business structure advice with company setup and PRO support. We can also assist with liquidation processes and Golden Visa matters when those are part of your plans. The exact steps depend on your business and the relevant authority.

## Support across the company lifecycle

- **Business structure advisory:** Discuss your activities, ownership and operating model so the available setup routes can be considered against your priorities.
- **Company setup and PRO services:** Organise formation information, application documents and the practical coordination involved in business setup and PRO processes.
- **Liquidation process assistance:** Work through the information and administrative steps needed when an existing business is closing, with the applicable authority requirements kept in view.
- **Golden Visa assistance:** Review the intended application route and supporting information, then coordinate assistance with the relevant submission process where appropriate.

## How an engagement begins

1. **Clarify the objective:** We discuss what the business will do, who will own it and what you need the structure to support.
2. **Map the requirements:** We identify the information, documents and decisions needed for the chosen route or corporate matter.
3. **Move the work forward:** We agree the scope of assistance and help coordinate the next practical steps.

## What to have ready

- Proposed business activity and operating model
- Ownership and shareholder information
- Preferred location or jurisdiction, if known
- Target timing and any existing company documents

Licensing, visa and liquidation requirements vary by authority and individual circumstances. TGA can help you understand the process, but approvals are decided by the relevant authority.
