# AML Risk Assessments & Policies in the UAE

> Connect the risks your business faces to policies and controls that people can actually use.

An AML framework should reflect the entity's customers, services, transactions, delivery channels and geographic exposure. A generic policy rarely explains how staff should handle the risks they see.
TGA can help review the business-wide risk assessment, document gaps and organise proportionate policies and procedures for management review.

## What we cover

- **Business-wide risk assessment:** Consider inherent risks, existing controls and areas needing attention.
- **Policies and procedures:** Document responsibilities, workflows and escalation routes for the team.
- **Control review:** Identify practical improvements and a schedule for future updates.

## How we work

1. **Map the business:** Understand services, customers, locations and transaction patterns.
2. **Review controls:** Examine existing policies and how they operate in practice.
3. **Set priorities:** Prepare a focused improvement plan for management review.

## Useful information to share

- Business activity and customer profile
- Existing AML risk assessment
- Policies, procedures and control records
- Prior review findings or regulator correspondence

Risk assessments and controls should be proportionate to the entity's actual exposure and applicable supervisory requirements. Management approval and accountability remain with the business.
