# Audit & Assurance Assistance in Dubai

> Prepare management records and responses for work carried out by an appointed auditor or assurance provider.

Category: Accounting, bookkeeping & audit support.

## Overview

An audit or assurance engagement runs more smoothly when the underlying records, schedules and explanations are ready to review. Management may need help locating documents or resolving questions raised during the process.
TGA offers audit and assurance assistance. We support the company side of the engagement by organising available information and coordinating follow-up. The independent auditor or assurance provider remains responsible for its own opinion or report.

## Where TGA can help

- **Record preparation:** Bring together ledgers, statements, reconciliations and supporting documents requested for review.
- **Schedules and explanations:** Help management prepare information that explains material balances or transactions.
- **Query coordination:** Track open requests and support timely responses to the appointed provider.

## How we work

1. **Review the request:** We clarify the reporting period and information needed.
2. **Organise materials:** We help prepare the schedules and records in scope.
3. **Support follow-up:** We assist management in responding to further queries.

## Useful information to share

- Audit or assurance request list
- Current and prior financial statements
- General ledger and reconciliations
- Relevant contracts and supporting records

This service is assistance to management. It does not represent an independent audit opinion or appointment as statutory auditor.
