# Banking & will registration assistance

Practical support with document preparation and coordination for bank account applications and will registration.

## What we cover

- [Bank Account Opening Assistance](https://www.tga-uae.com/bank-account-opening-assistance.html): Prepare company and ownership information for a bank application. The bank makes its own decision.
- [Will Registration Assistance](https://www.tga-uae.com/will-registration-assistance.html): Coordinate registration steps and documents with the relevant authority and a qualified legal adviser.
