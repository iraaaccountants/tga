Liquidation Process Assistance in the UAE

Organise the records, decisions and administrative steps involved in closing an existing business.

Discuss this service ↗

Overview

Support shaped around the matter at hand.

Closing a company is a process that needs coordination, not just a final form. The business may have records, contracts, registrations and outstanding matters to review before closure can be completed.

TGA lists liquidation process assistance among its services. We can help you map the applicable steps, gather the information needed for the relevant authority and coordinate the practical work within an agreed scope.

What we cover

Where we can help.

01

Current position

Review the company’s licence, registrations and available financial information to identify open matters.

02

Closure requirements

Organise the documents and confirmations requested for the selected liquidation route.

03

Process coordination

Keep the administrative stages and responsible parties visible as the work progresses.

How we work

A clear route from question to action.

  1. 01

    Review the company

    We discuss its legal and operating position.

  2. 02

    Map the steps

    We identify information and parties involved in the closure route.

  3. 03

    Support the process

    We assist with agreed documentation and coordination.

Getting started

Useful information to share

  • Trade licence and incorporation documents
  • Recent financial records and outstanding balances
  • List of registrations and material contracts
  • Shareholder decision or proposed closure date

Closure requirements differ by legal form, authority and the company’s outstanding obligations. The relevant authority makes the final closure decision.
