# POA Registration Assistance

Administrative support for preparing and registering a power of attorney through an appropriate UAE notary process.

## What we cover

- [Purpose & parties](https://www.tga-uae.com/poa-registration-assistance.html#poa-purpose): Organise the intended purpose, principal and attorney details for specialist review.
- [Document checklist](https://www.tga-uae.com/poa-registration-assistance.html#poa-documents): Gather identity and company documents requested for the selected notary process.
- [Notary appointment coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/poa-registration-assistance.html#poa-appointment): Help coordinate the application, signing appointment and follow-up requirements.

TGA provides application and document coordination. The relevant authority or qualified provider decides on approvals and legal documents.
