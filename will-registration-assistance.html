Overview

Practical help for the next step.

Understand the selected registration route and organise the documents and appointments involved.

TGA can help organise the information, documents and work needed for an agreed scope. We start by understanding your circumstances and the relevant process.

What we cover

Areas where we can help.

01

Registration route coordination

Identify the relevant registration authority and its current process with a qualified adviser.

02

Document organisation

Bring together identity, asset and beneficiary information requested for the matter.

03

Appointment coordination

Help track the application and any registration appointment or follow-up.

How we work

From requirements to action.

  1. 01

    Understand the request

    We discuss your objective, timing and information already available.

  2. 02

    Agree the scope

    We identify the documents and professional support needed for the matter.

  3. 03

    Coordinate the work

    We help prepare the agreed information and follow-up steps.

Getting started

Tell us what you need.

Share a short description of your business or matter, your target timing, and any documents or correspondence already received.

TGA provides administrative assistance. A qualified legal professional should advise on eligibility, drafting, legal effect and the appropriate registration route. The relevant authority decides whether to register the will.
