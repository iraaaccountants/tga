# Accounting & Bookkeeping Services in Dubai

> Keep transaction records organised so reporting, tax work and business decisions have a reliable starting point.

Category: Accounting, bookkeeping & audit support.

## Overview

Day-to-day accounting becomes more useful when transactions are recorded consistently and supporting documents can be found. Delayed or incomplete records make management reporting and later reviews harder.
TGA provides accounting and bookkeeping support for UAE businesses. We begin with the records and systems you already use, then agree the work needed to maintain or improve the information available.

## Where TGA can help

- **Transaction records:** Organise income, expenses and other entries with supporting invoices and documents.
- **Reconciliations:** Compare records with bank and other source information so differences can be investigated.
- **Reporting inputs:** Prepare a clearer base for management information, tax work and year-end reporting.

## How we work

1. **Review the records:** We identify gaps and the reporting periods involved.
2. **Agree a workflow:** We define how documents and questions will be shared.
3. **Maintain the information:** We complete the agreed bookkeeping and review steps.

## Useful information to share

- Recent ledger or accounting-system access
- Bank statements
- Sales and purchase documents
- Prior financial statements or reports

The scope depends on record quality, volume and the reporting outputs required. Historical clean-up can be discussed separately.
