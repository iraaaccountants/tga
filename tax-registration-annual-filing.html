Overview

Support shaped around the matter at hand.

Different taxes and business circumstances can create different registration or filing tasks. A useful starting point is to identify the entity, its activities, the tax period and what has already been submitted.

TGA supports tax registration and annual filing work. We help bring together company details, financial information and prior records so the applicable task can be reviewed and prepared. For VAT and Corporate Tax, see the dedicated service pages for more focused information.
