# TGA services

TGA groups related services into seven areas. Select an area or a specific service below.

## Business setup & company changes

Plan a new company, then keep its licence and corporate records current.

### Business setup

[Explore Business setup](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-formation)

- [Business structure advisory](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/business-structure-advisory): Consider how ownership and activities affect the setup route.
- [Company formation planning](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-formation#formation-planning): Map the decisions and documents for the chosen route.
- [Formation information](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-setup-pro-services): Organise shareholder, management and activity details.
- [Setup coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-formation#setup-coordination): Keep the agreed formation steps moving.

### Licensing & company changes

[Explore Licensing & company changes](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-licensing)

- [Mainland & free zone comparison](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-licensing#jurisdiction-comparison): Compare practical setup routes against your activities, location and operating plans.
- [Trade name reservation support](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-licensing#trade-name-reservation): Prepare preferred names and coordinate the reservation process with the relevant authority.
- [Licence activity selection](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-licensing#licence-activity-selection): Review proposed activities and identify the classifications to confirm with the licensing authority.
- [Branch setup support](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-licensing#branch-setup): Organise parent company details and documents for a proposed branch.
- [Trade licence renewal assistance](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-licensing#trade-licence-renewal): Track renewal timing, required documents and the relevant authority process.
- [Trade licence amendments](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-licensing#trade-licence-amendments): Coordinate requests to change shareholders, managers, address, activities or trade name.
- [Corporate document organisation](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-licensing#corporate-document-organisation): Keep licences, resolutions and company records organised for ongoing administration.

- [Company Formation & MOA Coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-formation#company-formation-moa): Organise formation documents and coordinate Memorandum of Association preparation with an authorised provider.
- [Government Approval Coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/services/business-setup/company-licensing#government-approvals): Track additional approvals requested by the relevant municipality, department or sector regulator.
