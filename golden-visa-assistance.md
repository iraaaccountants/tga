# Golden Visa Assistance in the UAE

> Understand the intended Golden Residency route and organise the evidence needed for an application.

Category: Business setup & PRO services.

## Overview

Golden Residency is available through different eligibility routes, each with its own evidence and application requirements. An applicant should first identify the route relevant to their circumstances and check the current official criteria.
TGA offers Golden Visa assistance. We can discuss the intended route, help organise supporting information and coordinate application-related steps within an agreed scope. Eligibility and approval remain decisions for the competent authority.

## Where TGA can help

- **Route discussion:** Identify the applicant category being considered and the questions that need official confirmation.
- **Evidence preparation:** List the identity, professional, investment or other supporting information relevant to that route.
- **Application coordination:** Help keep the agreed document and submission steps organised.

## How we work

1. **Discuss eligibility route:** We clarify which category the applicant intends to pursue.
2. **Review documentation:** We identify the information to gather or verify.
3. **Coordinate the next step:** We assist with the application process as agreed.

## Useful information to share

- Applicant identity documents
- The intended eligibility category
- Relevant professional or investment evidence
- Any existing UAE residency information

Golden Residency criteria and decisions are controlled by the UAE authorities. No adviser can guarantee approval.
