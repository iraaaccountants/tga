# International Transfer Pricing Support in Dubai

> Review cross-border related-party arrangements with attention to roles, evidence and documentation.

Category: UAE tax advisory & transfer pricing.

## Overview

When group companies in different countries transact with one another, the commercial arrangement and local documentation need to be understood together. Differences in functions, contracts and financial results can make a generic comparison misleading.
TGA lists international transfer pricing among its services. We can help map the cross-border transaction, gather available evidence and discuss the analysis or documentation needed for the UAE side of the matter.

## Where TGA can help

- **Cross-border mapping:** Identify the entities, flows and commercial purpose of each relevant arrangement.
- **Evidence and consistency:** Compare agreements, actual conduct and financial records across the parties.
- **Documentation support:** Organise the information needed to explain the pricing approach and open questions.

## How we work

1. **Define the arrangement:** We identify the transaction and reporting periods.
2. **Review the evidence:** We examine the contracts and financial facts available.
3. **Set the analysis scope:** We agree the documentation and further work required.

## Useful information to share

- Group chart and jurisdictions involved
- Intercompany agreements and invoices
- Financial information for each transaction
- Existing local or group transfer pricing work

Cross-border positions may be affected by requirements in more than one jurisdiction. Local advice may be needed outside the UAE.
