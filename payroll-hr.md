# Payroll & HR administration

Practical support for payroll calculations, records and reporting.

## What we cover

- [Payroll processing](https://www.tga-uae.com/payroll-hr#payroll-processing): Prepare periodic payroll calculations and payment information from approved employee records.
- [Leave & end-of-service calculations](https://www.tga-uae.com/payroll-hr#leave-end-of-service): Prepare calculations using the employee information and applicable terms supplied by the employer.
- [Employee record administration](https://www.tga-uae.com/payroll-hr#employee-records): Keep key payroll and employment information organised.
- [WPS file preparation & payment coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/payroll-hr#wps-support): Prepare payroll data and coordinate the agreed Wage Protection System payment workflow.
- [Payroll reporting](https://www.tga-uae.com/payroll-hr#payroll-reporting): Provide payroll summaries and reconciliations for management review.
