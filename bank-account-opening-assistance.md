# Bank Account Opening Assistance

Organise company, ownership and transaction information for a bank account application.

## What we cover

- [Application information](https://www.tga-uae.com/bank-account-opening-assistance#application-information): Bring together company, licence, ownership and authorised signatory details.
- [Document checklist](https://www.tga-uae.com/bank-account-opening-assistance#document-checklist): Organise the records requested by the selected bank.
- [Bank follow-up coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/bank-account-opening-assistance#bank-follow-up): Track requested clarifications and document updates during the application.
