Business Structure Advisory in Dubai

Make the early decisions about activity, ownership and operating model before choosing a UAE setup route.

Discuss this service ↗

Overview

Support shaped around the matter at hand.

A company structure should support what the business actually plans to do. Before an application is prepared, it helps to be clear about the proposed activities, where the business will operate, who will own it and what the owners expect to change later.

TGA’s business structure advisory starts with those facts. We help organise the questions that shape a setup decision and discuss the practical implications of available routes. The appropriate route depends on the activity and requirements of the relevant authority.

What we cover

Where we can help.

03

Setup route
