# TGA Manangement Consultancy LLC

TGA is a Dubai-based consultancy providing business setup, financial and compliance support for companies operating in the United Arab Emirates. Its website is https://www.tga-uae.com/.

## At a glance

- Seven or more years of experience in the UAE market.
- Ten team members listed on the website.
- Seven grouped service areas: business setup and company changes; PRO, visas and employee services; accounting, finance and payroll; tax and transfer pricing; compliance and governance; trade and IP; banking and will registration.

## Services

The home page highlights TGA's expertise in business setup, accounting, tax advisory and compliance, each with a dedicated photo and short introduction.

- **UAE business setup:** Business structure advice, formation planning and setup coordination.
- **PRO services:** Applications, corporate administration, liquidation assistance and Golden Visa assistance.
- **Accounting, bookkeeping and audit support:** Financial recordkeeping, accounting, audit and assurance assistance, and IFRS studies and impact assessments.
- **UAE tax, VAT and transfer pricing:** Tax registration and annual filing support, VAT and Corporate Tax registration and filing, tax advice, transfer pricing studies and benchmarking, and international transfer pricing.
- **AML compliance assistance:** Support for goAML registration, client KYC, UBO records, AML risk controls, screening, monitoring, STR/SAR reporting preparation and training where applicable.

Browse the [complete services and subservices directory](https://www.tga-uae.com/services.html), or read the full service pages: [business setup](https://www.tga-uae.com/business-setup.html), [PRO services](https://www.tga-uae.com/pro-services.html), [accounting and audit support](https://www.tga-uae.com/accounting-bookkeeping-audit.html), [tax and transfer pricing](https://www.tga-uae.com/tax-advisory-transfer-pricing.html), and [AML compliance](https://www.tga-uae.com/aml-compliance.html).
