# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. [Read the complete website policy](https://www.tga-uae.com/privacy.html).

TGA Manangement Consultancy LLC, Office 105, Al Jawhara Building, Mankhool, Dubai, UAE, is the point of contact for personal information connected with the public website and enquiries. The website has no account registration or on-site enquiry form.

When someone contacts TGA by email, telephone or WhatsApp, TGA receives the details they choose to provide. Hosting may record technical information needed to deliver and protect pages. Enquiry information is used to respond and manage any separately agreed engagement. Technical information may be used to operate and secure the website.

The site currently has no advertising pixels or analytics scripts and does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. It loads DM Sans and Manrope through Google Fonts. WhatsApp is an external service with its own privacy terms. Website hosting, email and messaging providers may process information, including outside the UAE.

Information is retained as reasonably needed for its purpose and applicable obligations. To ask about, correct or raise a concern about information relating to you, email [kintu@tga-uae.com](mailto:kintu@tga-uae.com). Applicable law and recordkeeping duties may affect requests. See the [UAE Government data protection overview](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws.).
