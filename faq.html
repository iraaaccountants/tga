Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about working with TGA and the business services we provide in the UAE.

Find a useful starting point.

These answers explain how TGA can help and what to prepare for a conversation. Requirements depend on your circumstances and current authority guidance. Follow the links for more detail, or contact our team.

01 / 04

Business setup

How do I choose a UAE business structure?+

Start with your planned activity, ownership, where you intend to operate and how the business will be managed. TGA can help compare practical setup routes and organise the next steps. Explore business structure advisory.

What can TGA help with during company setup?+

TGA can assist with the information and coordination involved in formation and PRO processes. The exact documents and authority steps depend on the selected jurisdiction and activity. Read about company setup and PRO services.

Can TGA help with a Golden Visa application?+

TGA can help discuss an application route and organise available documents. Eligibility and approval are decided by the relevant UAE authority. Review the ICP Golden Residency guide and TGA assistance.

02 / 04

Accounting and reporting

What information is useful when starting bookkeeping support?+

Your trade licence, existing accounts, bank statements, invoices, expense records and the period you need help with are a useful starting point. TGA can identify additional records after reviewing the scope. See accounting and bookkeeping support.

Does audit assistance mean TGA issues an audit opinion?+

The service described on this site is audit and assurance assistance, such as organising records, schedules and responses for an audit process. Confirm any separate statutory audit requirement and the identity of the appointed auditor for your company. Read the service scope.

When might an IFRS impact assessment help?+

An assessment can be useful when a transaction, accounting policy or reporting change may affect financial statements. TGA can help define the question and review available records. Explore IFRS studies and impact assessments.

03 / 04

Tax and compliance

How do I know whether VAT registration applies to my business?+

VAT registration depends on the business’s taxable activity and current Federal Tax Authority rules. TGA can help organise turnover and transaction information for a review. Check the FTA VAT registration guidance and our VAT support page.

Are Corporate Tax registration and filing the same task?+

They are separate steps: registration identifies the taxable person with the Federal Tax Authority, while a return reports information for a tax period. The obligations that apply depend on the entity and current rules. See FTA services and TGA Corporate Tax support.

What should I prepare for a tax filing discussion?+

Bring your trade licence or entity details, existing tax registrations, financial records, prior returns if available, and the filing period or deadline you are concerned about. Read about tax registration and annual filing.

What is transfer pricing benchmarking used for?+

It supports an analysis of pricing in related-party transactions by considering relevant comparisons and the facts of the arrangement. The approach and documentation depend on the transactions involved. Explore benchmarking assistance.

What does AML compliance assistance cover?+

TGA can help review available practices and records and identify areas that need further attention. Applicable obligations vary with business activities and regulatory status. Read about AML compliance assistance.
