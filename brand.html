TGA Branding

The identity behind our practical approach to business advice in the UAE.

Who we are

Accelerating Growth.

TGA Manangement Consultancy LLC supports businesses in Dubai and across the UAE with practical guidance on setup, accounting, tax and compliance.

Our brand reflects how we work: understand the business question, explain the options clearly and support the next action. “Accelerating Growth” expresses our aim to help clients move forward with informed decisions and dependable execution.

Purpose

What guides TGA.

01 / Mission

Advice that adds value.

Provide professional advice and practical support that serves clients’ interests.

02 / Vision

Better business outcomes.

Offer financial and business solutions that improve processes and support commercial goals.

03 / Approach

Make the next step clear.

Begin with the client’s context, define the work and explain the information needed to proceed.

Visual identity

A clear, confident blue.

The TGA identity uses deep blues for trust and structure, bright blue for energy and a light background to keep information easy to read.

Logo guidelines

Let the logo stay recognisable.

Use the supplied colour, white or black PNG artwork. Choose the version that reads clearly on the background, and keep its shape and space intact.

How we communicate

Professional. Direct. Useful.

We favour clear explanations and practical next steps over claims that cannot be supported.

01

Lead with the client’s need

Explain the business question before introducing a service.

02

Use plain language

Write so a decision-maker can understand the point on the first reading.

03

Keep claims specific

Describe the scope, process and known facts without promising a result.

Brand use

Keep the identity consistent.

Use the official assets on a clear, high-contrast background. Keep the logo’s proportions and colours; do not stretch, rotate, recolour or place it where the lettering is difficult to read.

Use the full company name TGA Manangement Consultancy LLC in formal descriptions and “TGA” where the context is already clear. Use “Accelerating Growth” as the brand tagline.
