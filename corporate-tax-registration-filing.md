# Corporate Tax Registration & Filing in the UAE

> Organise entity information, financial records and return questions for UAE Corporate Tax work.

Category: UAE tax advisory & transfer pricing.

## Overview

Corporate Tax registration and return preparation require a clear understanding of the entity, its tax period and its financial information. Unresolved accounting questions or incomplete supporting records can delay a meaningful review.
TGA offers Corporate Tax registration and filing support. We can help gather the relevant company details, review available financial information and coordinate preparation of the applicable registration or return work.

## Where TGA can help

- **Entity and registration details:** Identify the business documents and tax account information relevant to registration.
- **Financial information:** Bring together the statements and supporting schedules for the period in question.
- **Return preparation support:** Review the information needed for the return and highlight open tax or accounting matters.

## How we work

1. **Confirm the task:** We identify the entity, tax period and registration or filing matter.
2. **Gather the records:** We organise business and financial information.
3. **Review the submission:** We discuss open questions and the agreed filing support.

## Useful information to share

- Trade licence and incorporation documents
- Entity and ownership information
- Financial statements for the tax period
- Prior Corporate Tax filings or FTA correspondence, if any

Corporate Tax obligations, timelines and treatment depend on current FTA rules and the entity’s circumstances. Authority decisions and final responsibility remain with the taxpayer.
