# Will Registration Assistance

Understand the selected registration route and organise the documents and appointments involved.

## What we cover

- [Registration route coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/will-registration-assistance.html#registration-route): Identify the relevant registration authority and its current process with a qualified adviser.
- [Document organisation](https://www.tga-uae.com/will-registration-assistance.html#document-organisation): Bring together identity, asset and beneficiary information requested for the matter.
- [Appointment coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/will-registration-assistance.html#appointment-coordination): Help track the application and any registration appointment or follow-up.
