# Visa & Immigration Assistance

Practical application and document coordination for residence, family and visit matters.

## What we cover

- [Residence & Employment Visa Assistance](https://www.tga-uae.com/visa-immigration#residence-employment-visas): Organise application information and coordinate agreed medical, identity and residence steps.
- [Family & Dependent Visa Assistance](https://www.tga-uae.com/visa-immigration#family-dependent-visas): Prepare sponsorship information and supporting documents for eligible family members.
- [Emirates ID Application Support](https://www.tga-uae.com/visa-immigration#emirates-id-services): Coordinate new applications, renewals or replacements through the relevant authority process.
- [Visit & Tourist Visa Assistance](https://www.tga-uae.com/visa-immigration#visit-tourist-visas): Help organise information for an eligible visit or tourism entry permit application.

TGA provides application and document coordination. The relevant authority or qualified provider decides on approvals and legal documents.
