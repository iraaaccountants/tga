# Accounting, bookkeeping & audit support

> Clear records help you understand performance, answer reporting questions and prepare for the next business decision.

Keep the numbers useful.

Financial information is most useful when transactions are recorded consistently and reports can be understood by the people relying on them. Gaps in records make routine decisions, tax work and audit preparation harder than they need to be.
TGA offers accounting and bookkeeping support alongside audit and assurance assistance and IFRS studies and impact assessments. We start with the records you have, the reports you need and the deadlines that matter to your business.

## What this service covers

- **Accounting and bookkeeping:** Organise and maintain financial records so transactions are easier to review and reporting can be prepared from reliable information.
- **Financial reporting support:** Discuss the management and financial information needed by business owners and other stakeholders.
- **Audit and assurance assistance:** Help organise records, schedules and questions that arise during an audit or assurance process. This is assistance with the process, not a claim that TGA is the appointed auditor.
- **IFRS studies and impact assessments:** Examine how a reporting issue or change may affect financial information and identify matters that need further analysis.

## A practical reporting workflow

1. **Review the starting point:** We discuss the records, systems, reporting history and immediate gaps.
2. **Set priorities:** We agree which bookkeeping, reporting or audit support tasks need attention first.
3. **Prepare usable information:** We help bring the relevant records and analysis together for review and next steps.

## Useful starting information

- Recent bookkeeping or ledger records
- Bank statements and transaction support
- Prior financial statements or management reports
- Upcoming reporting or audit deadlines

The exact scope of accounting, audit assistance or IFRS work depends on the condition of the records and the purpose of the engagement.
