# Kintu Gandhi — Managing Partner at TGA

Kintu Gandhi is Managing Partner at TGA Manangement Consultancy LLC in Dubai. He brings more than nine years of experience across finance, audit, tax, corporate structuring, valuation and strategic advisory in the UAE and India.

## Professional background

- Chartered Accountant (ICAI)
- Company Secretary (ICSI)
- MBA in Finance
- B.Com in Accountancy from Gujarat University
- NSE certifications in the Financial Market, Derivatives Market and Capital Market

## Areas of experience

- Corporate finance, financial modelling and business strategy
- Business valuation, fundraising considerations, mergers and acquisitions
- Business structuring and company formation
- Audit, assurance and IFRS reporting matters
- Taxation and compliance
- Cross-border transactions and business questions involving India and the UAE
