# TGA Branding

TGA Manangement Consultancy LLC is a Dubai-based consultancy supporting businesses in the UAE with business setup, accounting, tax and compliance. Its tagline is **Accelerating Growth**.

## Purpose and approach

TGA aims to provide professional advice and practical support that serves clients' interests. Its vision is to offer financial and business solutions that improve processes and support commercial goals. The team starts with the client's context, explains options clearly and defines the next action.

## Visual identity

- Primary logo: [TGA logo with tagline](https://www.tga-uae.com/assets/tga-logo-transparent.png)
- Short mark: [TGA mark](https://www.tga-uae.com/assets/tga-mark-transparent.png)
- Core colours: Deep Navy `#08285A`; TGA Blue `#064DA8`; Bright Blue `#16A9E0`; Pale Blue `#E9F4FB`; White `#FFFFFF`.
- Website typography: Manrope for headings (usually weights 700–800) and DM Sans for body copy (usually weights 400–700). Use sentence case, a clear hierarchy and comfortable line spacing.
- Font downloads: [Manrope](https://www.tga-uae.com/assets/fonts/Manrope-font.zip) and [DM Sans](https://www.tga-uae.com/assets/fonts/DM-Sans-font.zip). Each ZIP includes the variable TTF font and its open font licence.

Use the supplied colour logo on light backgrounds and the supplied white logo on dark backgrounds. Keep its proportions, leave clear space and choose enough contrast. Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, redraw or add effects to the artwork. Request higher-resolution artwork for print. The [Media Kit](https://www.tga-uae.com/media-kit) includes downloadable web assets.
