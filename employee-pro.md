# Employee & PRO support

Document and application coordination for employee and company administration.

## What we cover

- [Establishment card support](https://www.tga-uae.com/employee-pro.html#establishment-card): Organise company information and supporting documents for the relevant establishment card process.
- [Employee visa application coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/employee-pro.html#employee-visa): Help prepare the information and document sequence for an employee visa application.
- [Work permit coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/employee-pro.html#work-permit): Coordinate information and documentation for the relevant work permit process.
- [Visa renewal & cancellation coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/employee-pro.html#visa-renewal-cancellation): Track the steps and documents for an agreed renewal or cancellation matter.
- [Emirates ID & medical appointment coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/employee-pro.html#emirates-id-medical): Help organise required appointment information and follow-up steps.
- [Document attestation & translation coordination](https://www.tga-uae.com/employee-pro.html#attestation-translation): Coordinate supporting documents with qualified translation and attestation providers.
