# PRO services in the UAE

Organise applications, documents and administrative steps connected to your UAE business.

Company administration often involves forms, supporting documents and coordination with different parties. The sequence depends on the matter and the relevant authority.

TGA helps gather information, organise the application file and coordinate agreed follow-up work. We also support specific Golden Visa and liquidation matters.

## What we cover

- [PRO coordination](company-setup-pro-services): Support practical administrative processes connected to the business and its approved setup route.
- [Applications &amp; documents](company-setup-pro-services): Organise forms, supporting documents and decisions for the agreed process.
- [Golden Visa assistance](golden-visa-assistance): Review the intended application route and supporting information.
- [Liquidation process assistance](liquidation-process-assistance): Organise information and administrative steps when an existing company is closing.
